1.5 Pumpkin Caramel

New

1.0 Sweet Vanilla

v1.0.0 · Android 17

The first ChangeOS. Android 17 with nothing you didn't ask for.

New

  • ChangeOS Launcher: date card, monochrome icons tinted by your accent, labeled dock, app drawer. Themes: Fog, Graphite and Loud.
  • System-wide accent: quick settings and Settings follow the color you pick.
  • Status chip: battery %, signal and Wi-Fi drawn as the ChangeOS ring.
  • Aurora Store and Firefox preinstalled, Termux as the built-in terminal.
  • Google Play is banned and removed if it ever shows up.
  • Debloated: stock browser, calendar, clock, gallery, music and search are gone.
  • Silent boot, no click sounds, and a boot animation where the ring draws itself in.

Known limits

  • x86_64 emulator only. The arm64 build for real phones is next.
  • In the emulator, a graphics-driver bug can restart the system when something reads screen pixels. Real phones don't have this driver.
  • Android 17's stock battery icon stays underneath the status chip until a custom SystemUI lands in 3.0 Banana Split.
